How are these people ranked?

Match score = 50% context match + 30% recent relevant activity + 20% relevant contributions. Each factor is scored from 0–100 in this fictional dataset. Contribution scores are capped at 10 distinct relevant documents; recent activity is measured as of 30 September 2026. Context scores are manually assigned for this scenario.

These are configurable prototype assumptions, not validated probabilities. A high match score means a promising person to ask; it does not guarantee they know the answer. The three shown are the highest-scoring people in a five-person demo pool.
